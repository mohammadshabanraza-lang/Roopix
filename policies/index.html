This page explains how the ROOPIX website and its enquiry links work. It is not a substitute for the transaction-specific terms displayed by an external store, supplier or logistics partner.

1. Privacy and enquiries

The ROOPIX website is currently an informational website. It does not provide website visitor accounts or a first-party payment checkout. When you choose to contact ROOPIX by email or WhatsApp, the information you include is shared with the selected communication provider and with ROOPIX so that the enquiry can be reviewed and answered.

Please send only information relevant to your request. Do not include passwords, payment card details or other sensitive information in an enquiry message.

ROOPIX uses the information voluntarily provided in a message to respond to the enquiry and discuss the requested product or service. The website code supplied for this release does not contain a first-party enquiry database or an analytics tracking implementation.

2. External platforms and links

Some links take visitors to third-party services, including the ROOPIX online store, WhatsApp, email services, Google Maps and social media. Those services operate under their own terms, privacy notices and technical practices. Please review the relevant provider's information before sharing data or completing a transaction.

ROOPIX does not control the availability or operation of third-party websites and services.

3. ROOPIX Beauty and retail purchases

Product assortment, shade options, stock, prices and delivery or pickup availability can change. Confirm the relevant details through the ROOPIX online store or directly with ROOPIX before placing an order.

Where a purchase is completed through the external online store, the seller identity, payment, delivery, cancellation, return and refund terms displayed on that platform and applicable to the order will govern that transaction. For an in-person purchase, please request the applicable purchase terms before payment.

This website does not publish a blanket return, refund, delivery-time or authenticity guarantee. Please rely on the applicable product label, seller details and confirmed order terms.

4. ROOPIX Exports and buyer enquiries

ROOPIX Exports presents product categories and sourcing coordination information for prospective business buyers. ROOPIX works with supplier partners and does not represent itself as the manufacturer of every product.

Product availability, material, dimensions, finish, MOQ, samples, customisation, inspection scope, packaging, lead time, price, shipping terms and documentation responsibilities are product- and order-specific. They are subject to confirmation with relevant suppliers and service partners and must be agreed before an order is placed.

Submitting an enquiry is a request for discussion, not an acceptance of an order or a binding quotation. A commercial offer and any resulting order are subject to separate written confirmation.

5. Website information and permitted use

Website text and images are provided for general information and may be updated as product ranges and business arrangements change. While ROOPIX aims to keep information useful and accurate, visitors should confirm current product and transaction details before relying on them.

Do not misuse the website, attempt unauthorised access, interfere with its operation or reproduce ROOPIX branding and content in a way that suggests an affiliation or endorsement without permission.
