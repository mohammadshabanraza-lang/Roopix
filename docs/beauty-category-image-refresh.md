# ROOPIX Beauty category image refresh

Updated the seven category cards with seven separately generated, category-specific editorial product still-life images:

- Lips: lipstick and lip gloss
- Face Makeup: foundation, concealer, powder and blush
- Eye Makeup: eyeshadow, mascara and liner
- Skincare: serum, moisturiser and cleanser packaging
- Accessories: brushes, sponges, tools and compact mirror
- Haircare: hair oils, treatment jars, combs and brushes
- Fragrance: perfume bottles

Images are original illustrative website visuals, not photographs of specific products currently in stock. They intentionally contain no brand names or ROOPIX product claims. Replace them with real SKU/brand photographs when approved stock photography is available.

The category layout is now four columns on wide desktop, three on medium screens, and two on mobile. The seven image-led cards replace the two previous text-only category tiles.
