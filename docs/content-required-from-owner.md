# ROOPIX content required before final commercial launch

The repository did not contain a complete export product dataset. The catalogue remains intentionally empty rather than showing invented products.

## ROOPIX Beauty
- Confirm current brand assortment and which brands/products are actively stocked.
- Supply product names, SKU/product codes, current prices, variants, images and accurate label information.
- Confirm the seller identity, order fulfilment arrangements and the policies displayed by the external online store.
- Confirm current store operating hours.

## ROOPIX Exports
- Supply product-specific photographs from multiple angles.
- Confirm each product's code, material, dimensions, weight, finish and packaging.
- Confirm MOQ, sample availability, customisation capability and lead time with the relevant supplier.
- Confirm who is responsible for quality inspection, export documentation, shipment and delivery in each transaction structure.
- Supply verified legal business name and registration information before any registration numbers are published.
- Supply actual company profile or registration documents if these are to be referenced publicly.

## Company and contact details
- Confirm the public-facing legal entity name and whether the current address is the registered or business address.
- Confirm phone / WhatsApp contact and official social profiles.
- Confirm whether a web form backend should be configured. The current export form uses a visitor-controlled email or WhatsApp handoff and does not store or transmit data to a website server.

## Legal review
- Have the privacy and website information page reviewed against the actual seller, entity, checkout and export transaction model before final publication.
- Add transaction-specific delivery, cancellation, returns and refund policies only after the actual terms are confirmed.
