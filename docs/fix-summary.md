# ROOPIX website update summary

See [`implementation-report.md`](implementation-report.md) for the complete change log, validation results and items that require hosting access or verified owner-supplied content.

This update reworks the homepage using the UX Pilot PDF as its design reference, clarifies Beauty and Exports messaging, improves mobile accessibility and reduces the visible Exports enquiry form to five fields. The existing static-site architecture and working external shopping/contact routes are retained.

No customer reviews, buyer statistics, certifications, product catalogue records, product specifications or domain email accounts have been invented. The Exports enquiry remains a visitor-controlled email/WhatsApp handoff until a secure server-side endpoint is configured.
