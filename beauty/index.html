Our Story

Helpful information

Before you shop.

A few practical answers to help you choose the right way to shop with ROOPIX Beauty.

Still have a question? Talk to us ↗
How can I check whether a product is available?

Product availability can change. Please check the online store or message ROOPIX on WhatsApp before placing an order.

Can I ask about shades or product suitability?

Yes. Share the product you are considering and the shade or finish you prefer. Our team can help with the information available to us.

Can I shop online?

Yes. The Online Store button opens the ROOPIX store hosted by our commerce platform. Store checkout, payment and order terms are presented on that platform.

Can I visit the physical store?

Yes. The ROOPIX outlet is listed at Sheesh Mahal Building, Ikonda Road, Joya, Amroha, Uttar Pradesh. Please contact us to confirm current store hours before travelling.

How do I ask about delivery, returns or refunds?

Please review the applicable terms shown during checkout on the online store, or contact ROOPIX before purchasing. The applicable seller and order terms govern each purchase.
