ROOPIX Exports — India

ROOPIX Exports coordinates Indian product sourcing through supplier partners for importers, wholesalers and retailers.

We discuss product specifications, finishes, quantities and order requirements with supplier partners. Where relevant, responsibilities for quality checks, packaging and export logistics are agreed for each order.

We coordinate sourcing of finished goods through supplier partners in India. ROOPIX Exports acts as a sourcing and export coordination partner, not as the manufacturer of every product.

Buyer questions

Useful details
before we begin.

We prefer clear conversations and order-specific confirmation over broad promises.

Discuss your requirement →
Does ROOPIX manufacture every product?

No. ROOPIX Exports works as a sourcing and export coordination partner with supplier partners in India. The manufacturing source and responsibilities are clarified for the relevant product and order.

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ depends on the product, supplier and any customisation required. It is confirmed after we understand your requirement.

Can I request samples or customisation?

Sample and customisation possibilities depend on the product and supplier. Share your request so we can check what may be feasible.

Can you share product specifications and prices?

Yes, available specifications and commercial details can be discussed for the selected product. Pricing, availability and lead times are confirmed individually and are not assumed to be fixed across products.

How are quality checks and packaging handled?

Quality expectations, inspection scope and packaging requirements are discussed and agreed for each order with the relevant supplier or service partner.

Can ROOPIX arrange export logistics?

Logistics and documentation responsibilities depend on the agreed transaction structure, supplier arrangements and destination. These are discussed before order confirmation.

How do I receive a quotation?

Use the enquiry form below or email roopix.in@gmail.com with your company, destination, product reference and estimated quantity. We will use the details to understand the requirement.
