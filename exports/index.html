About ROOPIX Exports

An exporter and sourcing partner

ROOPIX Exports works as a sourcing and export partner. We source finished goods from manufacturing and supplier partners in India and work with buyers to clarify what they need, so that the right product is confirmed before anything moves.

We are not presenting ourselves as the manufacturer of these products. ROOPIX Exports is a developing division of ROOPIX, and we would rather build each relationship carefully than overpromise.

"For us, growth is not only about expansion; it is about earning trust, improving every day and creating something that lasts."

Mohammad Shaban Raza, Founder, ROOPIX · Read the founder's note

How It Works

From enquiry to delivery coordination

A simple outline. The exact scope of each step is agreed with you for every order.

  1. 1

    Enquiry

    You share the product, quantity and any requirements.

  2. 2

    Requirement clarification

    We confirm specifications, finishes and expectations with you.

  3. 3

    Sourcing & product confirmation

    We source from supplier partners and confirm the product with you.

  4. 4

    Quality & inspection

    Coordination of checks, as agreed for the order.

  5. 5

    Packing & logistics

    Coordination of packing and shipping, as agreed for the order.
