ROOPIX EXPORTS — 2026

ROOPIX Enterprises connects international buyers with carefully sourced Indian metalware, handicrafts, bird feeders, wooden serving products and decorative home and garden items.

We work with supplier partners to confirm product specifications, finishes, quantities and order requirements before coordinating quality checks, packaging and export logistics.

We source finished goods through manufacturing and supplier partners in India; ROOPIX Exports works as a sourcing and export coordination partner rather than presenting itself as the manufacturer.

“ For us, growth is not only about expansion; it is about earning trust, improving every day and creating something that lasts.Mohammad Shaban Raza, Founder, ROOPIX
