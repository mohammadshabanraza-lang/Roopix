A Note from the Founder

Earning trust, every day.

ROOPIX began with a simple ambition: to build a brand people can trust, one that makes beauty more accessible while creating opportunities beyond our local market. What started as a beauty retail venture in Joya is part of a bigger vision: to build enduring businesses grounded in authenticity, quality, strong relationships and continuous learning.

Through ROOPIX Beauty, we help customers discover beauty and skincare with confidence. Through ROOPIX Exports, we aim to connect thoughtfully sourced Indian products and craftsmanship with buyers around the world. For us, growth is not only about expansion; it is about earning trust, improving every day and creating something that lasts.

Mohammad Shaban RazaFounder, ROOPIX
